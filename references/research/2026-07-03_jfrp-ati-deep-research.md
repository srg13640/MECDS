# Deep Research: JFRP / ATI Requirements-Process Changes (retrieved 2026-07-03)

Provenance: AI deep-research report (ChatGPT) commissioned by the project owner;
key claims independently spot-verified 2026-07-03 against jcs.mil (CJCSI 5123.01J,
CJCSM 5123.01) and army.mil (PAE announcement, acquisition-reform articles).
Used to update Capability Flow app content. Treat as a research snapshot, not policy.

## Key findings applied to the app

1. JCIDS replaced in its entirety by the Joint Force Requirements Process (JFRP),
   per CJCSI 5123.01J, "Charter of the Joint Requirements Oversight Council and the
   Joint Force Requirements Process," 15 Jan 2026, and CJCSM 5123.01 (same date).
   JROC reoriented to Joint Force Design, Joint Capability Integration, and CCMD
   requirements through Joint Operational Problems (JOPs). Service/component-level
   validation now rests with the Services. Previously validated JROC requirements
   and JROCMs remain in effect.
2. ACIDS (Army Capabilities Integration and Development System) is real and official
   under AR 71-9, "Warfighting Capabilities Determination," 29 Jun 2021 (still ACTIVE)
   — but the regulation implements JCIDS and is pending post-JFRP revision (public-
   policy lag).
3. AROC (Army Requirements Oversight Council) remains the Army validation forum under
   AR 71-9 (CSA-chaired, delegable); published composition predates T2COM/FCC.
4. JFRP artifacts: CRD (Capstone Requirements Document), JDCR (Joint DCR), CDR (CCMD
   Derived Requirement) per CJCSM 5123.01 Enclosure C.
5. Army artifacts under AR 71-9 remain: ICD, CDD, A-CDD, CDD-U, IS-CDD/RDP/CD,
   OCIERD, DCR/DICR, DR, CNS, ONS. CoN (Characteristics of Need) is an emerging
   problem-statement construct (NGC2/EMSO) not yet codified in AR 71-9.
6. Defense Acquisition System redesignated the Warfighting Acquisition System (WAS),
   SecWar memo 7 Nov 2025.
7. Six PAEs announced 14 Nov 2025: Fires (Redstone); Maneuver Ground (Ft Benning);
   Maneuver Air (Ft Rucker); Command and Control / Counter-C2; Agile Sustainment and
   Ammo; Layered Protection and CBRND. Dual reporting: ASA(ALT) for acquisition and
   materiel development; T2COM for requirements generation. PEOs transitioning to
   CPEs beginning Jan 2026.
8. Terminology: the Nov 2025 memo said "Key Operational Problems (KOP)"; governing
   term in law/JFRP documents is "Joint Operational Problems (JOP)."
